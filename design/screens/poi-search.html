Adding a place puts it in the wishlist — day_index stays null. Nothing is assigned to a day at capture time: the traveller has no basis to decide yet, and the planner would overrule them anyway. Distance is from the hotel, not the device — most people add places from the sofa, weeks before they fly, where “400 m away” would be meaningless.

The map keeps the sheet over the map rather than pushing to a detail screen: losing sight of where a place sits is the one thing this view exists to prevent. Pins encode state, not category — terracotta selected, green already added, grey not yet, dark the hotel — because when you are scanning for “what have I already got”, state is the question. OpenStreetMap attribution appears in both views because Nominatim’s terms require it, not because it looks tidy.
