TravelMate — palette directions

Two independent palettes. Pastel accents are chrome — surfaces, highlights, the eyecandy. Day colours are a data encoding: they carry “which day is this stop on” across map pins, timeline cards and route lines, so they must stay mutually distinguishable at 15px, which pastels cannot do. Pick one accent direction; the day ramp is shared.
